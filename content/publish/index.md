# Welcome to SteamEngine25

This is your digital garden powered by [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/).

## Getting Started

To add your Obsidian notes:
1. Copy your notes from your Obsidian vault to the `content/` directory
2. Commit and push the changes
3. GitHub Actions will automatically build and deploy your site

## Features

- 📝 Publish your Obsidian notes as a website
- 🔗 Automatic backlinks and graph view
- 🔍 Full-text search
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design
- 🌙 Dark mode support

Start writing your notes and watch them come to life!
