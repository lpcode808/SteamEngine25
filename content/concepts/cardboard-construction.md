# Cardboard Construction: Rapid, Low-Cost Iteration

> **"Props for testing, not perfection"** - How cardboard prototyping enables fast learning cycles and concept validation.

---

## Overview

Cardboard construction serves as the bridge between digital design ideas and final fabricated solutions. This accessible material allows for rapid testing of concepts, dimensional validation, and user experience evaluation without committing to expensive or time-intensive final materials.

## Strategic Use in Design Process

### Concept Confirmation
**[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-22|Day 22: Dimensional Planning]]**
*"Focus on the right dimensions rather than fine detail"*

Cardboard prototyping helps verify:
- Scale relationships and proportions
- Functional requirements and user interaction
- Assembly sequences and construction logic
- Spatial relationships between components

### Professional Development Model
**[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-22|iPhone Development Models]]**

Students learned how professional design teams use cardboard prototyping:
- Systematic progression from concept to product
- Multiple iteration cycles before material commitment
- User testing integration at low cost and time investment
- Team coordination around physical models

## Applications Across Projects

Both [[../projects/robot-storage|Robot Storage]] and [[../projects/dollhouse-design|Dollhouse]] used cardboard prototyping for dimensional validation and user testing before final fabrication.

## Material Science Learning

### Understanding Properties
- Cardboard characteristics: strength, flexibility, fabrication properties
- Design implications of material constraints and capabilities
- Testing protocols for evaluating material performance
- Professional connections between prototyping and industry practice

### Construction Techniques
- Joining methods that transfer to laser cutting design
- Structural principles that inform final material choices
- Assembly sequences that guide manufacturing planning
- Quality standards appropriate for testing vs. final production
