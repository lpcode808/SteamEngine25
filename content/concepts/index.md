# Core Concepts: The Ideas That Connect Everything

> **Frameworks for thinking, learning, and making** - The conceptual foundation that supports all STEAM learning.

## The Big Ideas

These aren't just theories - they're practical frameworks that guided every project, every challenge, and every breakthrough in our STEAM journey.

### **[[design-thinking|🎯 Design Thinking Process]]**
**Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test**

The human-centered problem-solving methodology that became second nature to our students. From [[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-01|Day 1's cardstock challenge]] to [[../projects/robot-storage|professional teacher solutions]], design thinking provided the structure for tackling any challenge.

**Key Applications:**
- [[../projects/family-coasters|Personal projects]] with family focus
- [[../projects/dollhouse-design|Cross-curricular collaboration]] with language learning
- [[../connections/community-engagement|Community presentation]] at Tech Week

---

### **[[4ms-framework|🔧 The 4 Ms Framework]]**  
**Maker · Machine · Method · Materials · Margin**

Systems thinking for makers - understanding how all elements interact to create successful (or unsuccessful) projects. Based on the Ishikawa diagram, adapted for educational making.

**Real Examples:**
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-27|Day 27]]**: Material thickness affecting design dimensions
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-31|Day 31]]**: Margin planning for complex geometry
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-34|Day 34]]**: Method evolution in CAD-to-fabrication workflow

---

### **[[ai-integration|🤖 AI as Creative Partner]]**
**Ethics first, capabilities second**

Learning to work with AI as a collaborator rather than a replacement for human thinking. Understanding both the power and the responsibility that comes with generative AI tools.

**Progression:**
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-20|Day 20]]**: Ethics and responsible use principles
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-28|Day 28]]**: "Vibe coding" - rapid app development
- **[[../learning-journey/consolidated#day-30|Day 30]]**: Advanced image generation and editing

---

### **[[reflection-methods|📝 Learning Through Reflection]]**
**"What was surprising, frustrating, accomplished, curious about?"**

The assessment philosophy that prioritizes growth mindset and process learning over perfect products. How students develop metacognition and become lifelong learners.

**Integration Across Projects:**
- **[[../tools-techniques/assessment-portfolio|Portfolio development]]** practices
- **Peer feedback** using "notice and wonder" protocols
- **[[../learning-journey/milestones|Milestone recognition]]** and celebration

## How the Concepts Connect

### **Design Thinking ↔ 4 Ms Framework**
- **Empathize** focuses on the **Maker** (user) and their needs
- **Define** clarifies requirements for all 4 Ms
- **Ideate** explores combinations of **Method**, **Machine**, and **Materials**
- **Prototype** tests concepts within **Margin** constraints
- **Test** evaluates whether **Maker** needs are met

### **AI Integration ↔ Design Thinking**
- **AI as ideation partner** in the Ideate phase
- **AI for research and synthesis** in Empathize and Define
- **AI for rapid prototyping** in the Prototype phase
- **Human creativity and ethics** guiding all AI collaboration

### **Reflection ↔ All Frameworks**
- **Process documentation** across all concept applications
- **Learning recognition** in both successes and challenges
- **Future application** planning for continued growth
