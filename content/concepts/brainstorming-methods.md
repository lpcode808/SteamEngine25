# Brainstorming Methods: Collaborative Idea Generation

> **"Build on others' ideas rather than competing with them"** - Techniques for productive group ideation.

---

## Overview

Effective brainstorming creates an environment where diverse perspectives combine to generate possibilities no individual would reach alone. Our STEAM course uses structured methods that balance creative freedom with productive collaboration.

## Core Principles

### "Yes, And..." Mindset
Building on ideas rather than evaluating them during generation phase.

### Diverse Perspectives  
Leveraging different backgrounds, interests, and thinking styles within the group.

### Quantity Before Quality
Generating many possibilities before evaluating which ones to pursue.

## Techniques Used

- **Silent start**: Individual generation before group sharing
- **Building rounds**: Each person adds to previous ideas  
- **Constraint brainstorming**: Using limitations to spark creativity
- **[[ai-integration|AI partnership]]**: Leveraging AI for possibility expansion
