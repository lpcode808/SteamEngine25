# AI Integration MOC

> [!tldr] TL;DR
> We use AI as a creative partner, not a replacement for thinking. Our core principle is **"Ethics first, capabilities second."** We use tools like **[[atlas/concepts/AI Magic School Bot|Magic School AI]]** and **[[atlas/concepts/AI Gemini Integration|Google Gemini]]** to brainstorm ideas, get feedback, and accelerate our work responsibly.

> [!quote] Why it matters
> AI is one of the most powerful tools of our time. Learning to use it ethically and effectively is a critical skill for any future career. This isn't about "cheating"; it's about learning to collaborate with intelligent systems to produce better work and learn faster.

---

## Key Ideas: Our Approach to AI

1.  **[[atlas/concepts/AI Integration#ethical-foundation|Ethics First]]**: Before using any tool, we learn the "Explain the Whole Process" test. If you can't explain how you got to the answer (including the AI's part), you haven't used it responsibly.

2.  **AI as a Collaborator**: We treat AI as a "word calculator, personalized tutor, or research assistant."
    -   **Brainstorming**: Using AI to generate a wide range of ideas during the **[[atlas/concepts/Design Thinking#ideate|Ideate]]** phase.
    -   **Feedback**: Asking AI for feedback on a design, as we did with Gemini for the [[efforts/Robot Storage|Robot Storage]] project.
    -   **Learning**: Asking AI to explain a concept we don't understand.

3.  **Critical Evaluation**: We never trust AI output blindly. We always discuss its limitations, like **[[atlas/concepts/AI Integration#understanding-limitations|"Context Rot,"]]** and the potential for bias, and we verify its suggestions.

## Activities & Assessment

-   **Activity**: Take a photo of a sketch for a project. Upload it to an AI like Gemini and ask for three suggestions to improve the design. Critically evaluate whether the suggestions are useful.
-   **Assessment**: In your **[[atlas/tools-techniques/Assessment Portfolio|Portfolio]]**, you should document when and how you used AI. Explain your prompts, the AI's suggestions, and your critical evaluation of the output. Show that you are the one driving the creative process.
