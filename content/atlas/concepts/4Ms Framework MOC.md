> [!tldr] TL;DR
> The 4Ms is a mental checklist for planning and troubleshooting any making project: **Maker, Machine, Method, Materials, Margin**. It helps you think systematically and avoid common problems.

> [!quote] Why it matters
> Ever had a project fail and not known why? The 4Ms gives you a powerful way to diagnose problems. Was it the person (`Maker`), the tool (`Machine`), the process (`Method`), the stuff you used (`Materials`), or did you not leave enough room for error (`Margin`)? It turns "It broke" into a solvable puzzle.

---

## Key Ideas: The 5 Components (Yes, 5)

The framework helps you analyze the entire system of a project.

1.  **[[atlas/concepts/4Ms Framework#maker|Maker]]**: The person doing the work.
    -   **Questions**: What are my skills? What are my learning goals? Who am I collaborating with?
    -   **See it in action**: In the [[efforts/Dollhouse Design|Dollhouse project]], the team's [[atlas/concepts/Group Cohesion|group cohesion]] (`Maker`) was the key to success.

2.  **[[atlas/concepts/4Ms Framework#machine|Machine]]**: The tools you use.
    -   **Questions**: What can this tool do? What are its limitations (e.g., the 2'x1' laser bed)?
    -   **See it in action**: Choosing the [[atlas/tools-techniques/Laser Cutting|laser cutter]] (`Machine`) for the [[efforts/Family Coasters|Family Coasters]] project defined the possible materials and precision.

3.  **[[atlas/concepts/4Ms Framework#method|Method]]**: The process you follow.
    -   **Questions**: What is my workflow? Am I using [[atlas/concepts/Design Thinking|Design Thinking]]? What's my plan for prototyping?
    -   **See it in action**: Our `Method` for learning [[atlas/tools-techniques/Onshape CAD|CAD]] involved [[atlas/concepts/Embedded Learning|observing a professional workflow first]].

4.  **[[atlas/concepts/4Ms Framework#materials|Materials]]**: The physical stuff you are working with.
    -   **Questions**: What are the properties of this material? Is it strong enough? Is it safe to laser cut?
    -   **See it in action**: We progressed from [[atlas/concepts/Cardboard Prototyping|cardboard]] to [[atlas/concepts/Plywood Projects|plywood]] (`Materials`) as our projects got more complex.

5.  **[[atlas/concepts/4Ms Framework#margin|Margin]]**: The buffer for mistakes, learning, and the unexpected.
    -   **Questions**: Do I have extra material for a failed cut? Is there slack in my timeline?
    -   **In action**: Planning for `Margin` in the [[efforts/Dollhouse Design|Dollhouse project]] meant we weren't panicked by the complex roof geometry on [[calendar/consolidated#day-31|Day 31]].

## Activities & Assessment

-   **Activity**: Before starting your next project, create a 4Ms plan. Write one sentence for each "M" detailing your approach.
-   **Assessment**: When you document a challenge in your **[[atlas/tools-techniques/Assessment Portfolio|Portfolio]]**, use the 4Ms to diagnose what went wrong. For example, "My joint didn't fit. The `Method` was right, but I didn't account for the `Material` thickness and the `Machine`'s kerf."
