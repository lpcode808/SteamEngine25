## Overview

Google Gemini represents the next evolution in AI creative partnership - a multi-modal AI capable of both analyzing and creating visual content with sophisticated understanding. Unlike text-only AI tools, Gemini can see, understand, and create images, making it an invaluable partner for design development and visual communication.

### **Visual AI Capabilities**

Image analysis, creative generation, design feedback, and visual communication support for design development.
