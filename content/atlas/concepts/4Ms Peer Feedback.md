# Peer Feedback: "Notice and Wonder" Presentation Protocols

> **"Constructive collaboration through systematic observation"** - How structured peer feedback builds communication skills and learning community.

**Practice Category**: Collaborative Learning and Communication  
**Primary Use**: Project sharing, learning community development, constructive evaluation  
**Key Methodology**: "Notice and wonder" framework for respectful and productive feedback

---

## Overview

Peer feedback in STEAM education goes beyond simple critique - it's a systematic methodology for building learning community, developing communication skills, and supporting each other's growth through structured observation and constructive input.

### **"Notice and Wonder" Feedback Framework**

Notice → Wonder → Appreciate → Suggest: Structured approach to constructive peer feedback and learning community development.

---

## Core Components

### **🤝 Constructive Communication Skills**

#### **Respectful Observation Protocol**
- **Descriptive rather than evaluative**: "I notice that..." instead of "This is good/bad"
- **Specific rather than general**: Focusing on particular elements and features rather than overall judgments
- **Learning-focused**: Emphasizing growth and development rather than performance evaluation
- **Curious questioning**: "I wonder if..." or "Have you considered..." to open possibilities rather than direct criticism

#### **Professional Communication Development**
- **Clear articulation**: Developing skills in explaining observations and suggestions clearly
- **Active listening**: Learning to receive and process feedback constructively
- **Constructive discussion**: Building skills in collaborative problem-solving and idea development
- **Respectful disagreement**: Understanding how to maintain relationships while expressing different perspectives

### **📊 Systematic Evaluation Skills**

#### **Design Assessment Framework**
- **Functional analysis**: Evaluating whether projects meet intended purposes and requirements
- **Technical execution**: Assessing quality of construction, materials use, and finishing
- **Creative effectiveness**: Understanding aesthetic choices and their impact
- **Learning demonstration**: Recognizing evidence of skill development and knowledge application

#### **Growth-Oriented Feedback**
- **Strength identification**: Recognizing successful elements and learning achievements
- **Development opportunities**: Identifying areas for continued growth and improvement
- **Resource suggestions**: Offering specific ideas for learning and development resources
- **Connection building**: Linking individual projects to broader learning goals and community knowledge

## Implementation Strategies

### **📋 Structured Feedback Protocols**

#### **Presentation Format**
- **Project context**: Brief explanation of goals, challenges, and intended outcomes
- **Process sharing**: Description of approach, decisions, and learning throughout development
- **Outcome evaluation**: Creator's assessment of success and areas for continued development
- **Question invitation**: Specific requests for input and feedback from peers

#### **Feedback Structure**
- **Observation sharing**: "I notice that..." statements about specific project elements
- **Curiosity expression**: "I wonder about..." questions that open possibilities for discussion and development
- **Appreciation statement**: Recognition of successful elements and learning achievements
- **Constructive suggestions**: Specific, actionable ideas for improvement and continued development

### **🔄 Continuous Improvement Integration**

#### **Feedback Application**
- **Prioritization skills**: Understanding which feedback to implement first based on available resources and time
- **Adaptation strategies**: Modifying suggestions to fit individual context, goals, and constraints
- **Integration planning**: Building feedback implementation into ongoing project development and learning
- **Follow-up sharing**: Reporting back to community on how feedback was used and what was learned

#### **Community Learning**
- **Pattern recognition**: Identifying common challenges and successful strategies across different projects
- **Resource development**: Creating shared knowledge base of techniques, solutions, and learning resources
- **Skill transfer**: Systematic sharing of individual learning for broader community benefit
- **Culture development**: Building habits and expectations that support ongoing collaborative learning and growth
