# Observation & Sketching: "I Notice and Wonder"

> **"Visual thinking foundation for design development"** - How systematic observation and sketching build design thinking and communication skills.

**Practice Category**: Visual Documentation and Analysis  
**Primary Use**: Design development, learning reflection, and communication  
**Key Principle**: "I notice and wonder" methodology for systematic observation and creative thinking

---

## Overview

Observation and sketching form the foundation of visual thinking in STEAM education. This practice goes far beyond artistic drawing - it's a systematic methodology for documenting learning, exploring ideas, developing design concepts, and communicating complex thinking effectively.

### **"I Notice and Wonder" Framework**

Notice → Wonder → Document → Connect: A systematic methodology for visual thinking and design development.

---

## Core Components

### **📝 Systematic Documentation**

#### **Learning Portfolio Foundation**
- **Date and context recording**: Establishing clear records for reflection and growth tracking
- **Process documentation**: Recording thinking development rather than just final outcomes
- **Visual thinking integration**: Using sketches, words, doodles, diagrams as interconnected thinking tools
- **Flexible organization**: "As orderly or messy as you'd like" - supporting different thinking and communication styles

#### **Design Development Process**
- **Concept exploration**: Using sketching to develop and refine design ideas through visual iteration
- **Problem analysis**: Drawing to understand complex challenges and identify potential solutions
- **Iteration planning**: Documenting design changes and improvement strategies systematically
- **Communication preparation**: Creating visual materials for effective sharing with others

### **👁️ Observational Learning**

#### **Professional Practice Integration**
- **Embedded learning**: Observing professional workflows before attempting hands-on practice
- **Pattern recognition**: Identifying recurring approaches, techniques, and decision-making strategies
- **Quality standards understanding**: Learning professional expectations through systematic observation
- **Skill development planning**: Understanding progression pathways through observation of expert practice

#### **Critical Analysis Development**
- **Functional evaluation**: Systematically assessing whether designs and solutions meet intended purposes
- **Aesthetic assessment**: Developing judgment about visual effectiveness and appeal
- **Technical analysis**: Understanding construction, materials, and process considerations through observation
- **User experience evaluation**: Observing and documenting how designs work for intended users
